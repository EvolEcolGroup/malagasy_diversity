# Admixture timing and population structure among the people of Madagascar 🇲🇬

# Context
The population of Madagascar exhibits a globally unique combination of African and Asian ancestries.  Malagasy African ancestry can be primarily attributed to Bantu sources, while Asian ancestry is derived from island southeast Asia (Pierron et al., 2014). Genetic admixture between these populations is estimated to have occurred between 500 and 900 years before present when Bantu populations encountered Austronesian communities (Pierron et al., 2014; 2017; Brucato et al., 2016). However, given limited genetic sampling of the Malagasy, elements of this admixture process remain unresolved. The precise timing of admixture, and whether multiple migrations contributed to the admixture observed among the Malagasy today, are still subject to debate.
